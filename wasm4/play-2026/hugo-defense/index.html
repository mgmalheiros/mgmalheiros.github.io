Hugo's Defense

Felipe Almeida Gomes
July 8, 2026

Welcome to Hugo's Defense! This is a unique hybrid game for the WASM-4 fantasy console that combines classic Tower Defense mechanics with a rogue-like restart, where if you die you got to start all over again.

Gameplay

Defend your base against waves of incoming enemies while keeping an eye on Hugo's Tower. Manage his Damage, Attack, and Health.

Survive through 10 waves of increasingly difficult bad goblins, culminating in an epic Boss Wave featuring the giant Grim Reaper!

Controls

  • Mouse/Click (or Touch): Interact with the UI buttons to buy upgrades for your tower and manage Hugo's stats.

Upgrades & Economy

Collect coins by defeating enemies to buy crucial upgrades for your defense:

  • DAM (Damage): Increases the damage dealt by your tower's projectiles.
  • SPD (Speed): Reduces the attack cooldown, making your tower shoot faster.
  • HP (Health): Increases your tower's maximum health to withstand more hits.

Good luck, and whatever you do, keep Hugo alive!
