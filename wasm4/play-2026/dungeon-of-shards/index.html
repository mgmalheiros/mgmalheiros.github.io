Dungeon of Shards

Giovana da Silva Bulcão
July 9, 2026
Cintia da Silva Bulcão

Legend has it that a cursed maze deep underground hides six ancient shards... and Kiro, a hunter with a taste for cursed loot (and bad puns), just couldn't resist the rumor. The catch? The dungeon is crawling with angry mobs that won't take kindly to a stranger poking around their treasure.

Inspired by the classic maze games, Dungeon of Shards drops you into Kiro's boots as you explore the dark corridors, dodge whatever's guarding them, and try to grab all six shards before something catches you first.

Fast reflexes and a good sense of direction are your best weapons. Good luck!!

How to Play

  • Collect the 6 shards scattered across the maze.
  • If an enemy catches you, it's game over.
  • Some enemies patrol fixed routes, but start chasing the player once he gets close.
  • The exit is a flashing tile at the far end of the maze, it only unlocks victory once all 6 shards have been collected. Step on it too early and you'll just get a warning telling you shards are missing.

Tip: the mobs guarding the dungeon aren't exactly geniuses... dodge them and grab the shards while they're not looking!

Controls

Button Action
Arrows Move through the maze
X Confirm / Retry after game over or win
