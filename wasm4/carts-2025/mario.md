# Mario

The game works as follows: You control a character who must move from left to right across the game map until reaching the end.

Along the way there are platforms and chasms; your character cannot fall into the chasms or they die. There are also some enemies on the map that kill your character if they touch them laterally, but your character can kill the enemies by jumping and landing on them.

The game uses the left and right directional buttons to move horizontally and the up directional button to jump. If your character dies or wins, you can press the spacebar to restart the game.
