# Desvie !
A simple game where the objective is to avoid cars that are randomly approaching on a road.

## In the menu
In the menu, you have to press Z to start the game.

## In the game 
The gameplay has simple mechanics: the directional buttons are used to move the character dodging cars.

## In the "game over" and "you win" screens
Press the X button to return to menu.
