# Cheddar

A rat, a dark sewer, and a whole lot of cheese. Explore room by room, eat your way past locked doors, and make your way deeper in.

It starts simple enough, but things aren't quite what they seem.

## Controls

- **⬅️ ➡️**: Move
- **⬆️**: Jump
- **X**: Confirm / advance
